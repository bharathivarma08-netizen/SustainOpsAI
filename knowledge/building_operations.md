# Building Operations and Energy Consumption

## Overview

Building energy consumption is influenced by operational schedules and
the way spaces and equipment are used.

Changes in occupancy, operating hours, equipment schedules, or building
activities can produce changes in energy demand.

## Investigation Context

When an energy anomaly is detected, investigators should consider whether
the building was operating under an unusual schedule.

Examples include:

- extended operating hours
- special events
- maintenance activities
- changes in occupancy
- equipment operating outside normal schedules

## Evidence Interpretation

Operational explanations should be treated as hypotheses unless supported
by operational records or other reliable evidence.

Energy meter data alone may not identify the exact operational event that
caused a deviation.

## Recommended Investigation

A building operator may review:

- operating schedules
- maintenance records
- occupancy information
- equipment schedules
- facility logs

These sources can provide additional evidence for or against a proposed
explanation.
