# Responsible AI for Energy Investigation

## Transparency

The system should clearly distinguish measured data, statistical
calculations, retrieved knowledge, AI-generated hypotheses, and
recommendations.

## Uncertainty

An anomaly indicates unusual behavior relative to a defined baseline.
It does not automatically indicate energy waste or equipment failure.

## Causality

Correlation between energy meters or similarity to a historical pattern
does not establish causality.

AI-generated explanations should therefore be described as plausible or
evidence-supported hypotheses.

## Human Oversight

Recommendations should be presented for review by an appropriate human
decision-maker.

The system should not autonomously control building equipment.

## Data Quality

Missing readings, insufficient historical observations, and limitations
of the baseline should be disclosed.

## Auditability

The investigation should record:

- incident identifier
- incident period
- evidence used
- retrieved knowledge
- hypotheses generated
- recommendation
- human decision
