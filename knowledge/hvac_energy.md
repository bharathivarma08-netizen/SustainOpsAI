# HVAC and Building Energy Consumption

## Overview

Heating, ventilation, and air-conditioning (HVAC) systems are important
components of building energy management.

Building energy consumption can vary depending on operating schedules,
thermal conditions, occupancy patterns, and the operation of heating or
cooling equipment.

## Investigation Context

An unusual increase in electricity consumption may coincide with changes
in heating or cooling-related meter behavior.

Supporting meter data can therefore be examined when investigating an
electricity anomaly.

However, correlation between electricity consumption and another meter
does not by itself establish that the supporting system caused the
electricity anomaly.

## Evidence Interpretation

When investigating a suspected energy anomaly:

1. Compare the supporting meter with its historical behavior.
2. Check whether the change occurs during the same incident period.
3. Consider the building's operating schedule.
4. Examine other available evidence.
5. Treat the result as a hypothesis unless causal evidence is available.

## Responsible Use

AI-generated explanations should be presented as plausible or
evidence-supported hypotheses rather than confirmed root causes.

Final operational decisions should be reviewed by an appropriate
facility manager or building operator.
