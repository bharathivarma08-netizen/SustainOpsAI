# Lighting and Equipment Energy Use

## Overview

Lighting systems and electrical equipment contribute to building
electricity consumption.

Their energy use can depend on operating schedules, occupancy, equipment
usage, and whether equipment remains active when spaces are not normally
occupied.

## Investigation Context

When electricity consumption is unexpectedly high, investigators can
consider whether lighting or equipment operation may have changed.

Examples include:

- equipment operating after normal hours
- lighting remaining active during unoccupied periods
- additional equipment being used
- changes in equipment schedules

## Evidence Interpretation

These factors are possible explanations rather than confirmed causes.

An AI system should not claim that lighting or equipment caused an
anomaly without supporting evidence.

Operational records and facility inspection can provide stronger evidence.
